# Initial independent observations — frozen before opening prior review reports

Date: 2026-11-17. Source: 577aad8a8dee0c60e3b7e949f6b8f7cf200fe180.

Read so far: saved project/schematic connectivity from fresh KiCad XML; saved PCB pads/tracks/zone polygons; project symbol/footprint tables and socket footprint; parts/interface inventory; TI ADS1299, TXU0304 and TPS7A24 datasheets; P1 connector and Waveshare Nano drawings. Parts inventory contains short historical review annotations; these were encountered while identifying MPNs. No earlier review reports or historical validation conclusions have been opened. This is a hypothesis log, not a final defect list.

1. Fresh KiCad 9.0.2 ERC and full DRC with schematic parity/all-track checks/exclusions included report zero violations, zero unconnected items and zero parity issues. No CAD edits or zone refill yet.
2. All 64 U1 connectivity assignments agree with the datasheet pin table, including RESV1=GND, NC27/29 and reserved64 open, CLKSEL via 10k to DVDD, unused clock open and GPIO pull-downs. Channel P inputs go to the recording sockets, all N inputs to REF_FILTERED. This is valid with SRB switches disabled, not an accidental polarity reversal. Firmware is needed to make it work.
3. Shared reference loads eight ADC N inputs through the same 20k/1nF filter used for one P input. Datasheet typical input capacitance implies unequal total loading; channel/reference tolerance and electrode imbalance need quantitative common-mode conversion analysis. This is not evidence that the design cannot acquire EEG.
4. The 20k series resistance on each leg adds real thermal noise. BAV199 orientation is correct (1 ground, 2 AVDD, 3 signal), but its forward drop is not a guaranteed AVDD+0.3V clamp. Both its rail injection and the ADC internal clamps must be included, especially when power is off. Estimate current paths before assigning severity.
5. Two 499k bias resistors limit only the bias contact. They do not protect all recording/reference paths under a rail-to-input fault. The common digital ground reaches Nano USB ground. TXU buffering is explicitly not galvanic isolation. Body-use readiness cannot follow from ERC/DRC.
6. U4/U5 directions and dual supplies agree with TXU0304; OE and ADC controls have low defaults. Both ADC supply rails arise from separate enabled regulators. TXU only observes DVDD and HOST_3V3, so an AVDD brownout with DVDD/host still valid is not detected by this hardware. Need state-by-state analysis and firmware/bench tests.
7. TPS7A24 fixed-part pin4 may be grounded; that is permitted, not a pinout defect. Dropout, fuse drop, dissipation and output-to-input reverse current during collapse require calculation. D10 is input polarity protection, not an output-to-input reverse-current clamp.
8. Saved PCB uses four layers with a continuous In1 ground fill and routed In2 ground fill. Local U1 bypass/VCAP/reference routes exist; AVDD1 has a local capacitor and distinct feed. Do not call them poor merely because they are longer than ideal. Quantify local loops and identify actual plane gaps under digital traces. B.Cu reference trunk and multiple reference vias need inspection for coupling, not automatic rejection.
9. P1 socket drawing Rev H and actual custom footprint need a full tolerance/fit comparison: 2.10mm signal hole and 4.20x2.10mm plated dummy slots are much larger than nominal legs, apparently intentionally. Physical Sino-k mating is still unverified. Verify affordable fabrication and solder/mechanical support before committing to ten sockets and a PCB batch.
10. Hand assembly of 0.5mm TQFP and 0.65mm TSSOP is feasible with appropriate equipment; footprints and orientation must be checked independently. No manufactured board, firmware, recorder, noise measurements, loop stability measurements or body-safety verification exist.
