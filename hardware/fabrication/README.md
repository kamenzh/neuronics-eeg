# Manufacturing release gate

A0 has no order-ready fabrication files. Do not use a PCB viewer's ability to
export Gerbers as evidence that the board is complete.

After the named design blockers close and schematic/layout reviews pass,
freeze a tagged revision and export Gerbers, Excellon drills, exact MPN BOM,
component placement/CPL, assembly drawings and stack-up/fabrication notes.
Inspect Gerbers independently and compare JLCPCB/PCBWay delivered quotes to
Bulgaria, including assembly, parts, shipping and tax.
